# CountDown31 
OFFICIAL GAME RULES

## Rule #1 — Stay Alive
Your objective is simple:
Be the last player remaining.

---

## Rule #2 — Follow the Turn Order
Play only when it is your turn.
The server controls the player order throughout the tournament.

---

## Rule #3 — Every Round Starts at 1
Every new round always begins with Number 1.

---

## Rule #4 — Continue the Count
You must always begin with the next available number.

---

## Rule #5 — Choose Your Count
On your turn you may announce:
• 1 Number
• 2 Numbers
• 3 Numbers
No more.

---

## Rule #6 — Don’t Repeat the Previous Digit Count
You cannot use the same Digit Count as the player immediately before you.
Repeating the previous Digit Count results in immediate elimination.

---

## Rule #7 — Keep Numbers Consecutive
Numbers must always be consecutive.
Skipping numbers is not allowed.

---

## Rule #8 — Think Fast
You have 5 seconds to complete your turn.
Time runs out…
You’re out.

---

## Rule #9 — Never Say 31
If you announce 31…
You’re Eliminated.

---

## Rule #10 — New Round
After someone is eliminated by saying 31,
The round immediately restarts from 1.

---

## Rule #11 — Stay in the Tournament
Only surviving players continue into the next round.

---

## Rule #12 — Last Player Wins
When only one player remains,
The tournament ends.
Champion.

---

## SKILLS

### Rule #13 — Pick Your Skills
Choose 2 Skills before the tournament starts.
Choose wisely. You can purchase skills in the shop.

---

### Rule #14 — One Use Only
Each Skill may only be used once per tournament.
Once it’s gone…
It’s gone.

---

### Rule #15 — Skills Work Only Until 21
Skills can only be activated while the current number is between 1 and 21.

---

### Rule #16 — Skill Lock
Once the count reaches 22,
All Skills become locked.
No exceptions.

---

### Rule #17 — New Round Unlock
When the count returns to 1,
Unused Skills become available again.
Used Skills never return.

---

### Rule #18 — Your Turn, Your Skill
Skills may only be activated during your own turn.

---

## FAIR PLAY

### Rule #19 — Play Fair
No Bots.
No Scripts.
No Macros.
No Exploits.
No Cheating.

---

### Rule #20 — One Account Only
One player.
One account.
Multiple accounts are prohibited.

---

### Rule #21 — Disconnects
If you fail to reconnect before the reconnect timer expires,
You are eliminated.

---

### Rule #22 — Spectator Mode
Once eliminated,
You become a spectator.
Watch.
Cheer.
Enjoy.

---

### Rule #23 — Server Is Always Right
All gameplay is validated by the official game server.
Server decisions are final.

---

---

### Rule #25 — No Match Fixing
Collusion, intentional losing, or manipulating results is strictly prohibited.

---

### Rule #26 — Tournament Rankings

---

### Rule #27 — Prize Distribution
