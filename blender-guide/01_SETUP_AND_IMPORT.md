# 📖 Chapter 1: Setup, Navigation & Importing the Cow

In this chapter, we will prepare Blender's workspace, learn the only 4 navigation keys you need, import "Ben The Cow", and turn on viewport textures so he isn't plain grey!

---

## 🧹 Step 1: Start Fresh (Clean the Default Scene)

When you open Blender, you will see a splash screen with some artwork.

1. **Left-click anywhere** on the screen to close the splash popup.
2. In the middle of the screen, you will see a grey Cube, a Camera, and a Lamp.
3. Press **`A`** on your keyboard (this selects all objects — they will all light up with an orange outline).
4. Press the **`Delete`** key (or press `X` on your keyboard $\to$ click **Delete**).
5. **Result**: Your 3D grid is now completely clean and empty!

```
[ Default Cube + Camera + Light ] ➔ Press 'A' ➔ Press 'Delete' ➔ [ Clean Workspace ]
```

---

## 🕹️ Step 2: Master the Viewport Navigation

Place your right hand on your mouse:

* **To Look Around (Orbit)**: Click and hold down the **Scroll Wheel (Middle Mouse Button)** and move your mouse around.
* **To Slide Left/Right/Up/Down (Pan)**: Hold the **`Shift` key** on your keyboard, then click and hold the **Scroll Wheel** and move your mouse.
* **To Zoom In / Out**: Roll the **Scroll Wheel** forward and backward.
* **If you get disoriented or lost**: Press **`Numpad .`** (period on numpad) or press **`View ➔ Frame All`** at the top menu to center your view!

---

## 📥 Step 3: Import "Ben The Cow"

1. Look at the very top-left menu bar: Click **`File`**.
2. Hover your mouse down over **`Import`**.
3. Choose the format of your downloaded cow file:
   - If your file ends in `.glb` or `.gltf` $\to$ Click **`glTF 2.0 (.glb/.gltf)`**
   - If your file ends in `.fbx` $\to$ Click **`FBX (.fbx)`**
   - If your file ends in `.obj` $\to$ Click **`Wavefront (.obj)`**
4. A file browser window will pop up:
   - Navigate to your folder where you downloaded Ben The Cow.
   - Double-click on the file to import it.
5. **Result**: Ben The Cow will now appear right in the center of your 3D grid!

---

## 🎨 Step 4: Turn On Real Colors & Textures

By default, Blender shows models in "Solid Grey" mode. Let's turn on his real fur, nose, and body colors:

1. Look at the **top-right corner of the 3D viewport** (just above the 3D compass colored sphere with X, Y, Z).
2. You will see **4 small circle sphere icons**:
   ```
   [ 1: Wireframe ]   [ 2: Solid Grey ]   [ 3: Material Preview ⭐ ]   [ 4: Rendered ]
   ```
3. **Click the 3rd circle** (Material Preview).
4. **Result**: Ben The Cow will immediately display in full color!

---

## 🎯 Step 5: How to Select the Cow

* Move your mouse cursor over the cow and **Left-Click** on his body.
* Notice the bright **orange outline** around him — this means the cow is selected and active.
* If you click on the empty grey background, the orange outline disappears (deselected). Click him again to re-select!

---

## 🛑 Troubleshooting / "Help, I got stuck!"

> [!TIP]
> **Issue: The cow is gigantic or microscopic!**  
> *Fix*: Select the cow $\to$ press **`S`** (Scale) $\to$ move your mouse to make him bigger or smaller $\to$ **Left-click** to confirm.

> [!TIP]
> **Issue: I made a mistake or moved something by accident!**  
> *Fix*: Press **`Ctrl + Z`** to undo!
