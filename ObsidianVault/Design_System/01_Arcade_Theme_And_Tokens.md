# 🎨 Arcade Theme & Visual Tokens

## 1. Color Palette
- **Primary Arcade Amber**: `#f59e0b` (Glows, Rim Highlights, Pointer Arrow)
- **Active Emerald Green**: `#10b981` (Interactive Turn Cards, Selection Badges, Confirm Move Button)
- **Opponent Magenta / Rose**: `#f43f5e` / `#ff43c4` (Bessie AI, Opponent Turn Glow, 31 Danger Bomb)
- **Background Pasture Gradient**: Deep pasture tones with radial dark vignettes.

---

## 2. Brass Rim & Machine Housing
- **Top / Bottom Brass Rims**: Heavy golden brass gradients (`from-amber-700 via-amber-400 to-amber-700`) lined with 20 spherical brass rivets with inset highlights.
- **Machine Window**: Dark brushed metal glass with radial glow reflecting current count danger level.
